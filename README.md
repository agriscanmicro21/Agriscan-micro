🌱 AgriScan Micro

Catch the warning signs before the problem spreads.

AgriScan Micro is a farmer-focused crop monitoring prototype designed to help detect early warning signs before a crop problem becomes widespread.

Instead of only identifying a problem after obvious damage appears, AgriScan Micro focuses on small changes and risk indicators such as:

* 🍃 Changes in plant or leaf appearance
* 💧 Moisture conditions
* 🌧️ Recent rainfall
* 🌱 Crop growth stage
* 📍 Patterns in nearby plants
* 📊 Previous farm observations

✨ Key Features

* 🔍 Micro Alerts — highlights possible early warning signs
* 🕵️ Crop Detective — shows clues behind a risk instead of only giving a name
* 🗺️ Farm Risk Map — helps farmers see areas that may need checking
* 🌎 Multilingual Design — made with farmers who may not speak English in mind
* 📸 Crop Scanning — allows farmers to upload crop photos
* 📈 Farm History — keeps track of observations over time

🎯 Why “Micro”?

Micro represents the small things that can be easy to miss:

Micro-signs → Micro-changes → Micro-alerts → Micro-action

The goal is simple: notice small warning signs early and help farmers investigate before the problem becomes widespread.

⚠️ Prototype Notice

AgriScan Micro is currently a prototype demonstration. Its results are simulated and should not be treated as a confirmed agricultural diagnosis.
